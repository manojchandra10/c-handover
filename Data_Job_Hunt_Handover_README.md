## Visa facts that shape the search

Checked on 2 October 2026. General information only.

- **Graduate visa.** It lasts two years if you apply on or before 31 December 2026, and 18 months if you apply on or after 1 January 2027. You must apply before your Student visa expires, once the university has told the Home Office that you completed the course. The fee is £937 plus the Immigration Health Surcharge, usually £1,035 for each year. It lets you work in most jobs with no sponsorship.
- **Skilled Worker visa.** The general salary threshold is £41,700 or the going rate for the job, whichever is higher. GOV.UK also lists a lower £33,400 threshold for some applicants; new entrants, such as people switching from a Student or Graduate visa, are one group to check. Medium-skilled jobs can only be sponsored if they are on the immigration salary list or the temporary shortage list.
- **Data analyst jobs.** Data analysts (SOC 3544) are on the temporary shortage list only until 31 December 2026, and people sponsored through it cannot bring dependants. Degree-level roles such as data scientist, statistician, quantitative analyst and strategy analyst are the likelier route to sponsorship after that.
- **In practice.** Junior data analyst jobs are fine while I hold a Graduate visa. For staying longer, the best targets are employers whose listings say they sponsor, and degree-level roles.
