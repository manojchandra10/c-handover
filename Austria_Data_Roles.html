Items 21 and 34 on the shortage list

21

Techniker/innen mit höherer Ausbildung (Ing.) für Datenverarbeitung · technicians with advanced training in data processing

IT technicians at the level of the Austrian Ing. title. Graduates of a higher technical college (HTL, an upper-secondary school) earn it after three years of relevant work. This sits below degree level and assumes work experience you do not have yet.

34

Diplomingenieur/innen für Datenverarbeitung · graduate engineers in data processing

Computing graduates at master's level. Dipl.-Ing. is the Austrian master's title in engineering and computer science. Your MSc is at this level, so 34 is the right item for you.

Two things decide whether item 34 works. The AMS judges whether a data science MSc counts as training in data processing. Your machine learning, big data and databases modules help, and your maths BSc on its own would be a weaker match. The job must also be in that occupation, which suits a data scientist or ML post in a technical team better than a business reporting job.

The list changes every year. Applications from 1 January 2027 fall under the 2027 list, which had not been published on 4 November 2026. Item 33 covers Ing.-level technicians not listed elsewhere, at the same level as item 21.

How an application runs

  1. You get a binding job offer. Tell the employer early that you would need a Red-White-Red Card or an EU Blue Card.
  2. The employer files the application with the residence authority in Austria, or you file it in person at the Austrian Embassy in London. The employer completes a declaration about the job.
  3. The AMS checks your points, the pay and, where the route needs it, the labour market.
  4. The authority decides, normally within eight weeks of a complete file.
  5. You get a D visa to travel, which Indian passport holders need.
  6. You collect the card in Austria and register your address within three days of moving in. The card runs for two years.

Documents to gather

  • Passport valid for the whole stay, and a passport photo.
  • BSc and MSc certificates and transcripts. Indian documents need an apostille from the Ministry of External Affairs. Ask the embassy whether your Nottingham documents need a UK apostille as well.
  • An English test certificate, for either Red-White-Red Card route.
  • Your CV, the signed job offer and the employer's declaration.
  • Proof of somewhere to live in Austria and of health insurance. Ask the embassy when each one is due.
  • The fee. 2026 guides give figures from about €160 to €218, so check the current amount with the embassy.

Other routes to ask about

India–Austria Working Holiday Programme. Reported as launched in 2026. Sources disagree on its length (six months or a year), and the age limit and quota could not be checked. Ask the Austrian Embassy in New Delhi.

Changing jobs later. Since 7 August 2026, card holders can reportedly change employer after telling the authority, and keep the card through a short gap between jobs. Fragomen reported this; read the details before relying on it.

Embassy area. Austrian embassies generally take applications from people who live lawfully in their area. When the employer files in Austria, your UK status matters less.

Before you apply

English-speaking roles are scarce

Most junior data adverts in Austria ask for German. The roles below work in English or may do, and the much longer list of roles left out for German is at the bottom of the page.

Pay comes 14 times a year

Austrian salaries are monthly figures paid 14 times, with the two extra payments in summer and before Christmas. Adverts must state the collective-agreement minimum, and offers are often higher.

Ask the employer to file in Austria

The employer can file your Red-White-Red Card application with the residence authority in Austria. Say in your first message that you would need a Red-White-Red Card or an EU Blue Card.

Book an English test

Without a certificate you score 45 or 46 points, and the Red-White-Red Card needs 55. B1 or above adds 10, which is enough. The Blue Card needs no certificate.
