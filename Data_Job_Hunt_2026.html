Before you apply

Graduate visa deadline

If you hold a Student visa, apply for the Graduate visa on or before 31 December 2026 to get two years. Applications from 1 January 2027 get 18 months. It covers most jobs with no sponsorship needed.

Graduate visa on GOV.UK

Sponsorship later on

Data analyst jobs (SOC 3544) can only be sponsored through the temporary shortage list until 31 December 2026. Data scientist, quantitative and strategy analyst roles are the likelier path to a Skilled Worker visa. This is general information and not immigration advice.

Skip "Training Course" ads

On Reed, ITOL Recruit, Newto Training, Uptrail and IT Career Switch show "Training Course" where a salary should be. They sell courses, so none of them appear here.

Use the careers service

Nottingham graduates keep lifelong access to the Careers and Employability Service. That covers MyCareer vacancies and CV feedback.
